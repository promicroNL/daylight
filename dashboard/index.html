About the data & how to read these charts

Faint lines or bars show daily observations. Stronger lines show the selected trailing calendar-day average using available readings only, including days before the visible range. Missing days remain gaps. Sleep-stage bars show the available stages without smoothing; absent stages are not estimated. Daily average cards weight available days equally.

Heart rate is the mean of recorded samples, not a time-weighted average. Resting heart rate uses the dedicated source. HRV is RMSSD. Active zone minutes are intensity-weighted as exported. Total and active calories are separate measurements. Skin temperature is not core body temperature; its baseline deviation may be negative.

Dates retain the calendar date in the source timestamp, without timezone conversion. Sleep duration and stages sum distinct sessions ending on each date; scores remain one value per date. Newer Google sources take precedence over legacy copies for each metric/day. Newer session days replace legacy session days to avoid double counting migrated IDs.

Hover or focus a chart and use arrow keys to inspect readings. Expand its daily table for exact values. Missing values and source-specific invalid readings are excluded; valid activity zeros are preserved. This is a personal history viewer, not a medical assessment.
